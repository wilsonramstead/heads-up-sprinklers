Our Specialty

Sprinkler Repair & System Revival

The heart of what we do. Broken and clogged heads, leaking or busted pipes, dry spots, weak coverage, valves that won't shut off — we diagnose the whole system and bring it back to life. Before anyone talks about a full replacement, we find out what your existing setup can still do. More often than not, we save it — and save you a lot of money.

  • Broken & clogged head replacement
  • Leaking & busted pipe repair
  • Dry-spot & coverage fixes
  • Valve & timer troubleshooting

New Irrigation Installs

A complete sprinkler system designed around your yard — efficient zones, even coverage and no wasted water. No frills, no sales pressure, and priced well below the big guys.

Zones, Heads & Coverage Tuning

Adjusting spray patterns, swapping heads and re-balancing zones so every corner of the lawn gets water — and the sidewalk doesn't. We come back as many times as it takes to get it perfect.

Valves, Timers & Controllers

Sticking valves, dead zones and confusing timers sorted out and set up right — so your system waters on schedule and shuts off when it should.

Emergency & Leak Repair

A busted sprinkler pipe flooding the yard can't wait. When something breaks, we find a way to fit you in — quick, efficient and reasonably priced.
